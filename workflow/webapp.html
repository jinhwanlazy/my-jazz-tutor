    About the live display

    Blue marks show when you press keys during playback; tails show key holds, not pedal sustain. This is a live view of the current pass, not a score of your performance.

    Staff placement follows the assigned notes or pitch range; it does not identify your hand. Notes outside the visible range are labeled at the edge. Dashed tails end at a pause or disconnect.
